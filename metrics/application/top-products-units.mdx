## How it's calculated

```sql
SUM(CASE WHEN trim(dimensions->>'quantity') matches a finite numeric value THEN quantity::numeric ELSE 0 END)
```

### Built from

- `Line item quantity`

## Known caveats

- Custom and deleted products can group by their retained line-item title.
