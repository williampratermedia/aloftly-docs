## How it's calculated

```text
(sum(shopify.gross_sales) + sum(shopify.discounts)) / nullif(sum(shopify.sales_orders), 0)
```

### Built from

- `shopify.gross_sales`
- `shopify.discounts`
- `shopify.sales_orders`

## Known caveats

- Ratio of sums; never average daily AOV rows.
