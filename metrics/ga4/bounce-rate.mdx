## How it's calculated

```text
(sum(ga4.sessions) - sum(ga4.engaged_sessions)) / nullif(sum(ga4.sessions), 0)
```

### Built from

- `ga4.sessions`
- `ga4.engaged_sessions`

## Known caveats

- Returns null until the engaged-session coverage guard is complete.
