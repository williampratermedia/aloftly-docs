description: "Aloftly is the unified CRO intelligence layer for Shopify. Connect every on-site optimization tool once, see cross-source signals in one briefing, run evidence-backed A/B tests, and prove the ROI of your CRO work in dollars."
---

Aloftly pulls data from every on-site optimization tool you already use — Shopify, Google Analytics 4, Microsoft Clarity, Intelligems, KnoCommerce, Gorgias, and Judge.me — into one place. It reads across those sources to surface what changed and why it matters, helps you turn that evidence into structured A/B tests, and ties every experiment back to revenue.

If you run CRO for client stores, or run your own program on a store doing serious volume, Aloftly replaces the tab-switching ritual of checking each tool separately with one view that has already done the cross-referencing for you.

Aloftly unifies **on-site CRO signals** — heatmaps, A/B tests, surveys, support sentiment, reviews. It is not a marketing-attribution tool; it covers the layer those tools don't touch.

## What you can do with Aloftly

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Connect your CRO stack once" icon="plug">
    Each integration connects in a few clicks and syncs into one normalized data model. After that, you stop logging into seven tools to understand one store — Aloftly holds the combined picture.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Start from a briefing, not a wall of charts" icon="newspaper">
    The **Overview** page reads across every connected source and leads with what changed, what it means, and what to do about it. Insights carry their evidence with them, so you can verify before you act.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Run a structured experimentation program" icon="flask">
    **Experiment OS** takes you from research to prioritized test ideas to running experiments. Ideas are ICE-scored, tied to the evidence that motivated them, and tracked through to results.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Build the dashboards your workflow needs" icon="table-columns">
    Assemble dashboards from your cross-source data instead of being forced into one fixed layout — per client, per store, or per program.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Prove your work paid for itself" icon="chart-line">
    Experiments tie to revenue contributed. For agencies, that means reporting that shows clients what the optimization program returned — in dollars, not screenshots.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Built for teams that run CRO across stores

Aloftly is agency-first: switching between client stores, scoping data per client, and client-ready presentation are core to how it works — not add-ons. Store owners running their own program get the same unified view for a single storefront.
