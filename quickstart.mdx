---

This guide takes you from a new account to your first unified view of a store's CRO signals.

<Note>
  Aloftly is in early access. If you don't have an account yet, email [support@aloftly.com](mailto:support@aloftly.com) to request access.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to Aloftly">
    Go to [app.aloftly.com](https://app.aloftly.com) and sign in, or create your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your organization">
    Your organization is the top level of your Aloftly account — it holds your stores, team members, and settings, and gets its own URL. If you're an agency, one organization covers all your clients; you'll group client stores inside it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your Shopify store">
    Open **Integrations** and connect **Shopify** first. Shopify provides the baseline — orders and revenue — that every other signal is read against.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the rest of your stack">
    Each additional integration adds a layer to the same picture:

    - **Google Analytics 4** — traffic and on-site behavior
    - **Microsoft Clarity** — heatmaps and session signals
    - **Intelligems** — A/B test results and revenue lift
    - **KnoCommerce** — post-purchase survey responses
    - **Judge.me** — product reviews
    - **Gorgias** — support ticket sentiment

    Connect the tools you use; Aloftly works with a partial stack and gets sharper with each source you add.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your Overview">
    Once your first sync completes, the **Overview** page becomes your starting point: what changed across your connected sources, what it means, and what's worth acting on.
  </Step>
</Steps>
